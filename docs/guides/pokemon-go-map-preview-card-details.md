# Pokémon GO Map: Preview Card Details

<!-- summary: Shows the exact coordinates of PokéStops, Gyms and Routes. Click to copy. -->

Shows the **exact coordinates** on the pop-up card when you click something on the [Pokémon GO map](https://pokemongo.com/gamemap).

## How to use it

1. Click a PokéStop, Gym, Power Spot or Route on the map.
2. Under its name on the card, you'll see its coordinates in grey, e.g. `(35.658600,139.745400)`. For routes, this is the starting point.
3. **Click the coordinates to copy them.** The text briefly changes to say they've been copied.

There are no settings.

> **Double-check before relying on them:** for places with no photo, or two places with the same name, the coordinates can occasionally belong to the last place you opened.
