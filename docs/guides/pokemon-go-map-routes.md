# Pokémon GO Map: Routes

<!-- summary: Adds a Routes button that shows Pokémon GO Routes on the map, with distance and duration. -->

Finds **Pokémon GO Routes** and shows them on the official [Pokémon GO map](https://pokemongo.com/en/map).

> The Pokémon GO map now asks you to **sign in with your Pokémon GO account** before it shows anything. Sign in first, and the add-on works once the map appears.

## How to use it

1. Open the [Pokémon GO map](https://pokemongo.com/en/map).
2. With the map's other filter buttons (Gyms, PokéStops…), click the new **Routes** button.
3. **Zoom in to level 16 or closer.** If you're too far out you'll see "Zoom to level 16 or closer to load Routes."
4. Each route appears as a green **S** pin (start) and a red **E** pin (end).
5. Click a pin to see the route's **name**, **distance**, **duration**, and whether it can be walked in both directions. The path is drawn on the map as an orange line.
6. Close the popup with **×**.
7. Click **Routes** again to turn it off.

**"Too many map cells…"**: you're looking at too big an area, so zoom in a little.

There are no settings.
