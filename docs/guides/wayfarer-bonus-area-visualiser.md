# Wayfarer Bonus Area Visualiser

<!-- summary: Shows on the Settings map exactly which area your Home or Bonus location covers. -->

Shows which area of the map your **Home** or **Bonus** location covers, so you can see where your nominations count.

## How to use it

1. In Wayfarer, click your **profile picture** (top right), then **Settings**.
2. Choose **Location**.
3. On the **Hometown** map, **nine light-green squares** outlined in black appear around your pin: the square under the pin, plus the eight around it. The map zooms out once so you can see all of them.
4. Scroll down to the **Bonus** map to see the same squares around your Bonus pin.
5. If Wayfarer lets you pick a new spot, the squares move with the pin.

In the far north (above about 60° latitude) the squares are bigger, matching how Wayfarer works there.

The squares only show on these Settings maps. There are no buttons or settings.

**Squares not showing?** Close Settings and open it again. If that doesn't help, reload the page.
