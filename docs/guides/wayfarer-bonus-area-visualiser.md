# Wayfarer Bonus Area Visualiser

<!-- summary: Shows on the Settings map exactly which area your Home or Bonus location covers. -->

Shows which area of the map your **Home** or **Bonus** location covers, so you can see where your nominations count.

## How to use it

1. In Wayfarer, go to **Settings**.
2. Look at the map where you set your location. **Nine light-green squares** outlined in black appear around your pin: the square under the pin, plus the eight around it.
3. Move the pin and the squares move with it.

In the far north (above about 60° latitude) the squares are bigger, matching how Wayfarer works there.

There are no buttons or settings.

**Squares not showing?** Reload the page. This can happen after using the browser's Back button.
