# Wayfarer Old Rating UI

<!-- summary: Shows your old Poor / Fair / Good / Great reviewer rating on your Profile page. -->

Shows your old **Wayfinder rating** as a four-part bar on your Profile page: **Poor · Fair · Good · Great**.

## How to use it

1. Open your **Profile** in Wayfarer.
2. In the credibility section, just above the newer credibility bar, you'll see the four-part bar with your rating highlighted.
3. Hover over the highlighted part to see a short message.

It follows Wayfarer's light/dark mode automatically. There are no settings.

## "Niantic has now hidden the old rating data…"

If you see this message instead of the bar, Wayfarer has stopped sending the old rating, and this add-on can't show it any more. You can uninstall it.
