# Wayfarer Old Rating UI

<!-- summary: Shows your old Poor / Fair / Good / Great reviewer rating on your Profile page. -->

Shows your old **Wayfinder rating** as a four-part bar on your Profile page: **Poor · Fair · Good · Great**.

Wayfarer still works out this rating for every reviewer, but the Profile page no longer shows it. It only shows the newer Wayfinder Tier (Novice to Champion). This add-on puts the old rating back.

## How to use it

1. Open your **Profile** in Wayfarer.
2. In the **Wayfinder Tier** box, just under your tier name, you'll see **Wayfinder rating** with your rating highlighted.
3. Hover over the highlighted part to see a short message.

It follows Wayfarer's light/dark mode and your Wayfarer language automatically. There are no settings.

## "Wayfarer no longer shares the old rating…"

If you see this message instead of the bar, Wayfarer has stopped sending the old rating, and this add-on can't show it any more. You can uninstall it.
